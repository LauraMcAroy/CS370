Connect your learning from throughout this course to the larger field of computer science:
What do computer scientists do and why does it matter?
Computer programs are everywhere and have become integrated into our daily lives. Computer scientists are the people that design, develop, and release those programs out into the world for countless numbers of people to use. Creating beneficial, quality programs is important because it affects so many people around the world.

How do I approach a problem as a computer scientist?
I break a problem down in to small steps, and complete each small step until I have created a larger project. The hint included in the given code were very beneficial in creating the Q-learning algorithm.

What are my ethical responsibilities to the end user and the organization?
To create trustworthy programs that are beneficial to both the organization and the user, that minimize bias and protect the users' data.
